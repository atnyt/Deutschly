Look up a German word, inspect dictionary IPA, and play recorded pronunciation when the source provides it. Missing data is shown honestly—not guessed.

About the results

The app queries Wiktapi's structured Wiktionary data. IPA and recorded audio vary by word. A device voice is offered separately as synthesized speech, not as a native-speaker recording.

This is a study aid, not a certified pronunciation assessment. Regional and context-dependent variants may exist.

Sources: Wiktapi documentation · Wiktionary. Browser and device support may vary.
